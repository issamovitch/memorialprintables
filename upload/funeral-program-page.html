What to include in a funeral program

A clear program helps guests follow the service and becomes a lasting keepsake.

◷ What to include

  • Full name, birth and death dates, and a cover photo.
  • The order of service — readings, hymns, eulogy, prayers.
  • A short obituary or life story.
  • Acknowledgments thanking those who offered support.
  • An optional poem, scripture, or favourite quote.

◷ Why it matters

A well-structured program gives mourners something to hold and follow, guides them through each moment of the service, and preserves the details of a life so families can return to it long after the day itself.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to make a program?▾
No. There is no signup, watermark, or hidden fee. Fill in the fields and download your PDF.
Is my data private?▾
Yes. Everything is generated in your browser. Your photo and details are never uploaded or saved.
What paper should I use?▾
Standard 8.5 × 11" letter paper works well. For a keepsake feel, use a heavier 80 lb cardstock.
How do I print a bifold?▾
Print double-sided on one sheet, then fold in half. The maker positions the panels so the cover lands on the front automatically.
Can I add my own photo?▾
Yes. Use the Upload photo button — it appears on the cover instantly and stays on your device.
