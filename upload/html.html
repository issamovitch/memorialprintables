Life Story

Margaret Eleanor Hayes was born in Asheville, North Carolina, and lived a life full of warmth, patience, and quiet generosity. She spent thirty years teaching, tended a garden her neighbors still admire, and never let a friend leave her table hungry. She is survived by her children, her grandchildren, and a community that loved her dearly.
